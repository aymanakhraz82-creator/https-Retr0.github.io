About InvoiceKit

A small, fast invoice generator for people who work for themselves. No account, no subscription, no invoice watermark.

Why this exists

Invoicing should take five minutes. Yet most freelancers end up choosing between a spreadsheet template that breaks the moment tax is involved, a paid tool with a free plan that caps you at three invoices, or a full accounting suite that wants your bank login before it will print a PDF.

InvoiceKit is the opposite: one page, one form, one preview. You type, the invoice appears, you download it. There is no onboarding tour, no upsell modal and no email verification. It works on a phone during a commute and on a laptop at a kitchen table.

How it works

The whole application is a single HTML page with a stylesheet and a small JavaScript file. Calculations run locally in your browser: quantity times rate, subtotal, discount, tax, total. The preview is the same markup that gets handed to the PDF library, so what you see is what you export. Printing uses your browser's own print dialog, which also gives you "Save as PDF" as a fallback.

Your last-used details — business name, client, invoice number, currency, tax rate, notes and logo — are stored in your browser's localStorage. That means the form is still filled in when you come back tomorrow. It also means the data is tied to that browser and that device. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching computers will start you from a blank invoice.

Privacy by default

There is no backend and no database. Invoice details and logos are never transmitted to us, because there is nowhere for them to go. The only network requests the page makes are for the stylesheet, the script and the PDF library. Advertising and affiliate partners are described in the privacy policy.

Who it is for

Designers, developers, writers, photographers, consultants, tutors, translators, tradespeople — anyone who sends a handful of invoices a month and would rather not pay for software to do it. It handles multiple currencies, percentage tax, fixed discounts and itemised work, which covers the vast majority of freelance billing.

What it is not

InvoiceKit is not accounting software. It does not store your invoices, track payments, chase late clients or file taxes. It does not provide legal or tax advice, and tax rules differ by country — check with a professional before relying on any template. If you need bookkeeping, the recommended tools section lists options.

Keeping it free

The site is supported by advertising and by clearly labelled affiliate links. Sponsored placements always carry a "Sponsored" label, and affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored". You never pay more by using them.
