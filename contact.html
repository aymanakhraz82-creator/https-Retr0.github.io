Reporting a bug

To help us reproduce the problem, include your browser and device (for example "Safari on iPhone 15"), the steps you took, and what you expected to happen. If the PDF export fails, say whether the Print button works instead. Please do not send real client data, bank details or tax identification numbers — screenshots of a test invoice are enough.

Feature requests

Ideas for the generator are welcome. It is deliberately small: no accounts, no backend, no tracking of your invoices. Requests that keep it fast and private are the ones most likely to be built.

InvoiceKit cannot help with unpaid invoices, legal disputes or tax filing. For those, speak to a qualified accountant or lawyer in your jurisdiction.
