How a browser-only tool works

1

Pick a tool and add your file

Your browser reads the file from disk. Nothing is sent over the network, so there is no upload bar to watch.

2

The work happens on your device

JavaScript libraries open and rebuild the file in memory. The privacy badge on each tool explains what runs.

3

Download the result

The generated file is handed straight back to you. Reload the page and every trace of it is gone.

Private by architecture

There is no backend to receive a file. That is a design choice, not a promise in a policy.

Fast, even on bad Wi-Fi

No upload means no waiting. A 30 MB PDF is processed at disk speed, not connection speed.

Free without a catch

No trial, account or export fee. Labelled ads cover hosting; the tools stay free.

Questions about the tools

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up, watermark or daily limit. The site is supported by clearly labelled ad slots and nothing else.

Do I need to upload my files?

No. All processing happens inside your browser tab using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your PDFs, photos and Wi-Fi passwords never leave your device, and most tools keep working offline once the page has loaded.

Can I use the tools on a phone?

Yes. Every page is mobile-first, with tap targets sized for thumbs and drag alternatives such as arrow buttons for reordering. Large files are easier on a desktop, but cropping, compressing and QR generation work well on phones.

Do you store anything about me?

No accounts, no file storage and no analytics profile. Your theme choice is stored locally in your browser. See the privacy policy for details about advertising cookies.
