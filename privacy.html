Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. This policy explains what happens to information when you use InvoiceKit.

Short version: your invoice content and logo stay in your browser. We do not have a server that receives them. Third-party advertising and affiliate partners may use cookies when ads or links are shown or clicked.

1. Who we are

InvoiceKit ("we", "us") operates this website, a free invoice generator for freelancers. This policy applies to the whole site, including the generator, the guide, the FAQ, the blog-style content pages and any advertising shown on them. If you have questions, use the details on our contact page.

2. Information you enter into the generator

Everything you type into the invoice form — your name or business name, client name, invoice number, dates, line items, tax and discount values, notes and an uploaded logo — is processed locally in your browser. It is not sent to us, stored on a server, or shared with a database. The same is true of the PDF generation: the PDF library runs in your browser and the file is created on your device.

To save you time, the last-used details are stored in your browser's local storage under a key named invoicekit:draft:v1. This is a feature of your browser, not a cookie we read on a server. The data stays on your device until you clear it, use the Reset button in the generator, or clear your browser's site data. On a shared computer you should reset the invoice or use a private window when you are finished.

3. Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves for the core tool. However, this site is supported by advertising and affiliate partnerships, and those third parties may set cookies or use similar technologies (such as web beacons or local identifiers) when their content is loaded or a link is clicked.

Cookies used for advertising help limit how often you see the same ad and can be used to show ads that are more relevant to you. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not prevent the invoice generator from working.

4. Advertising

This site displays advertising in marked slots. When those slots are filled by a third-party network such as Google AdSense, the network may use cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may be asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are set, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

Advertising networks may collect technical information such as your IP address, approximate location, browser type and the pages you view, in order to deliver and measure ads. We do not control those cookies and their use is governed by the relevant provider's privacy policy.

5. Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and then sign up for or purchase a product, we may receive a commission. This comes at no extra cost to you and does not change the price you pay.

Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow" and sponsored placements are labelled "Sponsored". When you click an affiliate link, the merchant or its affiliate network may set a cookie so it can attribute the referral, usually for a limited period. That cookie is controlled by the third party, not by us. We only list products we believe are relevant to freelancers, but you should always do your own research before buying.

6. Analytics and server logs

This is a static website. If it is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs for security and operational purposes, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. If we add a privacy-friendly analytics tool in the future, this policy will be updated to name it and explain what it measures.

7. How we use information

Because we do not receive the content of your invoices, we cannot and do not use it for any purpose. The limited technical information described above is used to operate the site, display advertising, measure whether advertising works, and understand aggregated traffic patterns. We do not sell personal information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent. Since we hold no server-side account data about you, most requests will concern data held by advertising or affiliate partners, and you should contact them directly or use the opt-out links above. You can delete all locally stored invoice details at any time with the Reset button or by clearing your browser's site data.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

This site is intended for people running a business or freelance practice. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Data retention

Invoice drafts remain in your browser's local storage until you remove them. Advertising and affiliate cookies persist according to the settings of the providers that set them. Server logs held by our hosting provider are retained according to their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the site, our partners or the law. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Donations

If you choose to support InvoiceKit through a donation link, the payment is processed entirely by the third party you select (for example PayPal or Buy Me a Coffee). InvoiceKit does not receive or store your payment details. That provider's privacy policy governs the transaction.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@example.com or use the contact page. Replace this address with your own before publishing the site.
